Who We Are

Knowledge that supports human understanding.

The World Without Words is an educational and research initiative dedicated to making knowledge about human communication, behaviour, influence and protection accessible to everyone.

We bring together ideas from communication studies, linguistics, psychology, sociology and human rights to help people understand interactions with greater awareness and care.

Our Vision

A world where individuals can recognise harmful communication, understand their rights, respect one another's boundaries and make informed decisions without fear, coercion or exploitation.

Our Mission

To make reliable knowledge about communication, manipulation, human behaviour, consent and human dignity accessible to all.

ResearchEvidence-informed knowledge and critical inquiry.
EducationAccessible learning for informed decisions.
ProtectionAwareness of dignity, rights and boundaries.

Our Commitments

Responsible knowledge. Human dignity.

We believe awareness must be grounded in evidence, context and respect for every individual.

Evidence over assumptions and unsupported claims.
Context and patterns over isolated gestures or phrases.
Respect for cultural differences in communication.
No victim-blaming; responsibility belongs to the person causing harm.
Safety, privacy and dignity in educational work.
Learning tools are not diagnostic tests or lie detectors.
