Who We Are

Knowledge in the service of humanity.

The World Without Words is an educational and research initiative dedicated to making knowledge about human communication and behaviour accessible to everyone.

We explore what people communicate through words, silence, expressions and actions. We aim to help individuals recognise harmful patterns, understand personal boundaries and make informed decisions.

Our Vision

A world where individuals can recognise harmful communication, understand their rights, respect one another's boundaries and make informed decisions without fear, coercion or exploitation.


Our Mission

To make reliable knowledge about communication, manipulation, human behaviour, consent and human rights accessible to everyone.

Our Signature Learning Feature

The Human Communication Decoder

Explore how communication can carry meaning beyond literal words. Learn to distinguish what you observe from what you interpret.

Learning insight

Educational note: No single gesture, expression, phrase or behaviour proves someone's intentions. Meaning depends on context, individual differences and patterns over time.
01 — ObserveIdentify what was actually said or done without adding assumptions.
02 — InterpretConsider different possible meanings and relevant context.
03 — VerifySeek clarification and consider patterns rather than isolated signs.
